# Selection - an evolving ecology

An agent-based community-ecology and evolution simulation. Grass grows on a
160 x 90 grid; animals carry four integer traits (legs, body, mouth, eyes)
and a continuous fat store; mating requires an exact trait match INCLUDING
diet, so a species here is not a label but a trait-identity - and every
mutation (5% of births: one trait shifts one unit, or the diet flips
between herbivore and carnivore) is a step toward reproductive isolation.

**Watch it run: https://worldbyjoe.github.io/selection-abm/**

Four founder herbivore species with random traits wake in the four corners.
Everything after that is emergent: the boom, the overgrazed bust, the
radiation into dozens of coexisting species, and - when a diet-flip lineage
finds its footing - predators.

## The rules that matter

- **Concealment predation**: a prey animal standing in grass taller than its
  own size (legs+body+mouth+fat) is caught only 10% of the time; on bare
  ground, 100%. An overgrazed commons is a killing field, fat is a
  visibility cost, and tall grass is worth fighting over.
- **Trait-identity mating**: partners must match in all four traits and in
  diet, and both must hold enough fat to build the offspring plus a reserve
  for themselves. Mate scarcity gives rare species - new predators above
  all - a real Allee effect: their growth stays suppressed until numbers
  cross the threshold where partners are findable.
- **The mouth-body gate**: a predator can only swallow prey whose body its
  mouth matches. A large enough grazer is too big to eat - a size refuge
  that evolution may or may not find.

## Reading the screen

Every species wears a colour mixed from its own traits as CMYK ink - legs
cyan, body magenta, mouth yellow, eyes black - so similar species look
similar and the community strip's colour gradients are trait gradients.
Carnivores are pulled toward red and ringed in red on the map. The strip
along the bottom is the whole run's community composition, compressed as
it grows: left edge founding, right edge now. The table lists the most
common species by trait values; predator rows name their top prey.

Keys: **n** new world · **p** pause · **i** diagnostics.
