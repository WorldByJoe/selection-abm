Ecology & Evolution Sim

Grass grows. Animals eat, breed, hunt and starve. Nothing here is scripted — every species you see arrived by mutation and stayed because it worked.

Default settings use the shipped rules with a randomly drawn set of founding species, so every run differs.

SELECTION — RUN SETUP

Everything here is fixed at step one. Leave it alone for a random world, or compose one deliberately.

Eight populations — a grazer and a hunter for each of the four corners, two populations per corner. Traits 0–10; body and mouth must be at least 1. A hunter needs a mouth wider than its prey's body or it cannot eat at all, and any lineage you want to survive needs a count of at least two. Set a count to 0 to leave that slot empty.
the landscape
Patch size is the correlation length in cells — small values give fine mottling, large values give broad prairies and barrens. Patch contrast 0 makes the land uniform; 1 uses the full growth range.
the rules
Provisioning is a species trait: f spends little on each newborn and F spends a lot, and the two do not interbreed. Disturbance hits a random patch on that interval - most events are small, a few are very large. Killing animals but sparing the grass rewards whoever recolonises fastest; stripping the grass too forces the colonists to live on what their parents gave them.
